===== SKIN CONDITIONS & ROUTINES =====

CONDITION: Acne
Description: Acne is caused by clogged pores, excess oil (sebum), bacteria (C. acnes), and inflammation.
Active Ingredients:
- Salicylic acid (BHA) 2%: exfoliates inside pores, unclogs blackheads and whiteheads — use in BOTH cleanser and night serum
- Niacinamide 10%: reduces inflammation, controls sebum, fades post-acne marks — use as DAY serum
- Benzoyl peroxide 2.5%: kills acne-causing bacteria — spot treatment only, use at night
- Azelaic acid 10%: reduces redness, kills bacteria, fades hyperpigmentation
- Retinol 0.025-0.05%: increases cell turnover, prevents clogged pores (night only, start after acne clears)
Avoid: heavy oils, coconut oil, alcohol-based toners, physical scrubs
Cleanser: salicylic acid 2% face wash for acne prone oily skin deep cleansing
Toner: BHA salicylic acid exfoliating toner for acne oily skin pore control
Serum Day: niacinamide 10% zinc serum for acne inflammation oil control
Serum Night: salicylic acid 2% BHA night serum for acne treatment pore exfoliation — NOT retinol for active acne
Spot Treatment: benzoyl peroxide 2.5% spot gel for active pimples
Moisturizer: oil free non-comedogenic gel moisturizer for acne skin
Sunscreen: SPF 50 PA+++ lightweight non-comedogenic sunscreen for oily acne skin
Morning Routine Order: Salicylic Acid Cleanser → Niacinamide 10% Serum → Oil-Free Gel Moisturizer → SPF 50 Sunscreen
Night Routine Order: Salicylic Acid Cleanser → BHA Toner → Salicylic Acid 2% Night Serum → Oil-Free Gel Moisturizer
Key Acne Rule: For active acne — use SALICYLIC ACID serum at night (not retinol). Retinol is for anti-aging, not active breakouts.

===== SKIN TONE CONSIDERATIONS =====

Light/Fair Skin Tone:
- More prone to sun damage, redness, rosacea
- SPF is critical - use SPF 50+ broad spectrum daily
- Avoid: harsh acids that cause redness
- Recommended: gentle barrier-supporting products
- Best brightening: vitamin C, niacinamide

Medium/Wheatish Skin Tone:
- Prone to hyperpigmentation and tanning
- Avoid: alcohol-based products, harsh chemical peels
- Recommended: niacinamide, alpha arbutin for even tone
- SPF 50 essential to prevent darkening

Dark Skin Tone:
- Very prone to hyperpigmentation and keloid scarring from acne
- Avoid: hydroquinone at high concentrations, strong peels
- Recommended: azelaic acid, kojic acid, vitamin C for brightening
- Gentle actives preferred to avoid post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation
- SPF 30-50 daily to prevent further darkening
- For dark skin with acne: niacinamide is especially important to prevent PIH

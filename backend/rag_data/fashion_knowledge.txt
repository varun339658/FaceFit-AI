===== FACE SHAPE STYLING =====

Face Shape: Oval
Characteristics: balanced proportions, slightly wider forehead, narrow chin
Works with: almost all styles
Best Necklines: round neck, V-neck, crew neck, polo, boat neck
Best Hairstyles: any
Accessories: any frame shape works
Fashion Notes: oval face is the ideal face shape — any outfit works. Focus on body proportions instead.

Face Shape: Round
Characteristics: equal width and height, full cheeks, soft jaw
Goal: create the illusion of length and angularity
Best Necklines: V-neck, deep V, scoop neck, open collars, lapels
Avoid: turtlenecks, round necks, horizontal stripes on top
Best Patterns: vertical stripes, long cardigans, long pendants
Fashion Notes: elongate the face with vertical elements. Avoid anything that adds width to the face.

Face Shape: Square
Characteristics: strong jawline, wide forehead, equal width throughout
Goal: soften sharp angles
Best Necklines: round collar, cowl neck, off-shoulder, wide V-neck
Avoid: square necklines, sharp angular collars, turtlenecks
Best Patterns: soft curves, florals, rounded elements
Fashion Notes: relaxed fits and soft fabrics soften the strong jaw. Avoid structured boxy silhouettes.

Face Shape: Heart
Characteristics: wide forehead, narrow chin, prominent cheekbones
Goal: balance upper and lower body, add volume at bottom
Best Necklines: sweetheart neckline, wide scoop, off-shoulder
Avoid: high volume puff sleeves, heavy shoulder padding
Best Bottoms: wide leg trousers, A-line skirts, flared bottoms
Fashion Notes: draw attention downward with interesting bottom wear. Keep tops simple.

Face Shape: Oblong / Long
Characteristics: long narrow face, high forehead
Goal: add width, break vertical length
Best Necklines: boat neck, square neck, horizontal details, turtleneck
Avoid: V-necks that elongate further, long pendants
Fashion Notes: horizontal elements create width. Turtlenecks work well.

===== SEASONAL / WEATHER STYLING =====

Indian Summer (March–June) — HOT and HUMID:
Fabrics: cotton, linen, khadi, rayon, bamboo fabric
Colors: white, cream, mint green, sky blue, light yellow, pastel pink
Avoid: synthetic (polyester), dark heavy fabrics
Men: linen shirts, cotton kurtas, chino shorts, sandals
Women: cotton kurtis, linen trousers, cotton crop tops, breezy dresses
Key styling rule: breathability first. Loose fits over body-con. Natural fibers only.

Indian Monsoon (July–September) — HUMID and WET:
Fabrics: quick-dry polyester, denim, cotton blend
Colors: darker colors hide water stains — navy, grey, dark green, black, brown
Avoid: suede shoes (ruin in rain), very light colors (show wet patches)
Men: denim jeans, cotton t-shirt, water-resistant sneakers
Women: shorter kurtas, crop trousers, ankle boots
Key styling rule: waterproof shoes mandatory. Roll up trousers. Skip white.

Indian Winter / North India (November–February) — COOL:
Fabrics: wool blend, cashmere, velvet, fleece, denim
Colors: deep jewel tones — burgundy, forest green, navy, camel, chocolate
Layering is key: base (t-shirt/shirt) + mid layer (sweater/cardigan) + outer (jacket/coat)
Men: sweater over shirt + chinos + boots + scarf
Women: knit dress + tights + ankle boots + trench coat
Key styling rule: layer smartly. Scarves and belted coats add structure and style.

South India / Year-round warm:
Focus on breathable fabrics and lighter colors year-round. Ethnic wear cotton is king.
Avoid: heavy synthetic party wear — opt for tissue silk or cotton silk for festive events.

Rule 3: Shoes in a neutral that complements (not matches) the outfit

- Full tuck: formal settings, trousers with a belt

The Color Story Rule: Each outfit tells a color story. Choose:
- 1 primary color (dominant — shirt or trousers)
- 1 neutral (complements — shoes or outer layer)
- 1 accent (accessories — watch, bracelet, bag)

===== STYLING PHILOSOPHY =====

The Perfect Stylist's Checklist (for every outfit):
1. Does the fit work for the body type? (Most critical)
2. Do the colors complement the skin tone? (Second most important)
3. Are there exactly 3 accessories? (Completeness)
4. Do the shoes match the formality of the outfit? (Grounding the look)
5. Is there a color story — primary + neutral + accent? (Cohesion)
6. Is the outfit appropriate for the occasion AND weather? (Context)
7. Does the outfit feel authentic to the person's style? (Personal identity)

A perfect stylist considers:
- The person's skin tone and what colors make them glow
- Their face shape and which necklines flatter them
- The weather and season (comfort is style)
- The occasion and what's appropriate vs. what stands out
- Their existing wardrobe (build on what they have)
- Their personality and confidence level
- Their budget constraints (style has no price tag)

The World-Class Stylist Rule: Never recommend an outfit without accessories. A great outfit without the right watch, bracelet, necklace, or shoes is unfinished. Accessories are what separate a look from a complete look.
